Interview Preparation Checklist
Prepare before, during and after your interview.

Before
[ ] Read the job description and research the employer.
[ ] Prepare 3-5 examples showing relevant skills.
[ ] Practise a short introduction and questions for the interviewer.
[ ] Confirm the time, format, location or meeting link.

On the day
[ ] Check your connection, audio or travel plan.
[ ] Have the job description, CV and notes ready.
[ ] Listen carefully and ask for clarification if needed.
[ ] Use specific examples and explain your own contribution.

After
[ ] Record questions asked and useful feedback.
[ ] Note the next step and expected timeline.
[ ] Send a brief thank-you or follow-up when appropriate.
[ ] Choose one thing to improve for the next interview.
