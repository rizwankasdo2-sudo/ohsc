Graduate Cover Letter Example
A fictional letter illustrating a clear opening, evidence and closing.

Example letter
Dear Hiring Team,
I am applying for the project assistant role. The opportunity to support organised, collaborative project delivery matches the work I enjoyed during my degree.

Evidence paragraph
For a student research project, I maintained a shared task tracker and coordinated weekly updates. Our group delivered its findings on time and presented a clear summary to the class. This strengthened my planning and communication skills.

Closing paragraph
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my organisation and willingness to learn could support your team. Thank you for considering my application.
Yours sincerely,
Alex Morgan (fictional example)

Make it your own
Replace the role, employer and evidence with truthful details.
Explain a specific reason for choosing the organisation.
Match your evidence to the role rather than using a generic letter.
