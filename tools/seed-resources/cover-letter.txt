General Cover Letter Template
A practical structure for a tailored one-page cover letter.

Contact and opening
[Your name] | [Email] | [Phone] | [Date]
Dear [Hiring manager name / Hiring team],
I am applying for [role] at [organisation]. I am interested because [specific reason].

Show your fit
In [role/project], I [relevant action] which resulted in [honest outcome].
This helped me develop [skill] that relates to your requirement for [requirement].

Close with a next step
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to [team goal].
Thank you for considering my application.
Yours sincerely, [Name]

Final checks
Name the right organisation and role. Include evidence rather than repeating the CV.
Remove all placeholders. Keep the letter focused and easy to read.
