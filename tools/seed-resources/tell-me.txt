Tell Me About Yourself: Answer Examples
Use a short, relevant introduction supported by honest evidence.

A useful structure
Present: your current role, study or direction.
Evidence: one or two experiences relevant to the job.
Future: why this role is a logical next step.

Fictional graduate example
I recently completed a business degree, where I enjoyed organising group projects. In our final research project, I maintained the task tracker and helped prepare a clear presentation. I am now looking for an entry-level project support role where I can use those skills and learn from an experienced team.

Fictional customer service example
I have experience helping customers understand their options and resolving everyday issues. I focus on listening, explaining clearly and following up on what I promise. This role interests me because it would let me support customers while developing deeper product knowledge.
