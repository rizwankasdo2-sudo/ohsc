LinkedIn Headline and Summary Examples
Adapt these fictional examples to describe your own experience and direction.

Headline examples
Graduate business professional | Project coordination | Research and communication
Customer service adviser | Clear communication | Practical problem solving
Career changer | Operations experience | Developing data analysis skills

Summary structure
Start with your current direction and relevant experience.
Describe two strengths with evidence.
Finish with the kind of work or connections you want to explore.

Fictional summary
I am a business graduate interested in supporting well-organised projects. During a student research project, I coordinated tasks and helped turn findings into a clear presentation. I enjoy making information easy to follow and helping a team meet its commitments. I am exploring entry-level project support opportunities.

Before publishing
Use accurate skills and achievements. Keep private information out of public text.
Review spelling, dates and consistency with your CV.
