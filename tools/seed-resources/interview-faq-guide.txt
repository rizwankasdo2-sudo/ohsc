Interview Preparation: Questions and Answers

Question
How should I prepare for an interview?

Answer
Read the role requirements, research the employer and prepare a few truthful examples of relevant skills. Practise explaining your own actions and results. Confirm the format, time and meeting details, and prepare questions for the interviewer.
