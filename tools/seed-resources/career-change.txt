Career Change Checklist
Explore a career change with evidence and manageable next steps.

Understand the change
[ ] Write down what you want to change and what you want to keep.
[ ] List interests, values and working conditions that matter to you.
[ ] Identify transferable skills with examples.

Explore options
[ ] Research typical work, entry requirements and skill gaps.
[ ] Speak with people in the field where possible.
[ ] Test an option through a small project, learning or volunteering.

Build a transition plan
[ ] Identify practical commitments, time and support needs.
[ ] Choose a realistic first action and review date.
[ ] Tailor your CV to show transferable skills.
[ ] Review evidence before making a major commitment.
