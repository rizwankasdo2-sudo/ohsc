Professional Development: Questions and Answers

Question
How do I plan my professional development?

Answer
Choose a skill that matters to your current role or next goal. Plan a learning activity and a practical way to use it. Keep evidence, ask for feedback and set a review date so you can adjust the plan based on progress.
