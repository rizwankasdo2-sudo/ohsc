Job Search Checklist
A step-by-step checklist to organise your job search.

Set your direction
[ ] Choose target roles and locations or working patterns.
[ ] List the skills and evidence you can offer.
[ ] Decide how you will track applications and follow-up dates.

Prepare your application
[ ] Tailor your CV to the role and check contact details.
[ ] Write a specific cover letter when requested.
[ ] Check the employer instructions, file types and closing date.

Apply and follow up
[ ] Save the job description and a copy of your application.
[ ] Record the role, employer, application date and status.
[ ] Follow up politely when appropriate and respect stated timelines.

Review weekly
[ ] Review which applications led to responses.
[ ] Identify one improvement to your CV, search or preparation.
[ ] Plan the next week and allow time for rest.
