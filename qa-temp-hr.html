Free Online HR Courses

Oxford Home Study Centre offers a focused collection of free online HR courses for people who want to understand recruitment, people management and the responsible use of workplace technology. You can also browse the complete course catalogue or compare subjects in the wider collection of free courses with optional certificates.

Each course in this collection is delivered online, is self-paced and has an estimated study time of 20 hours. Choose one course for a specific skill or complete several to explore different parts of human resource management.

Which HR Course Should I Choose?

Start with HR Management for a general introduction

The HR Management Short Course introduces the purpose of human resource management before examining recruitment, selection and staff development. It is the clearest starting point if you are new to the subject and want an overview rather than a specialist topic.

Choose Recruitment Skills for a closer look at hiring

The Recruitment Skills Short Course examines how recruitment operates and how staffing approaches can vary across permanent, professional and management roles. It is more specialised than the general HR course and is intended for learners who want to focus on recruitment activity.

Explore HR and AI for workplace-process automation

The HR and AI Course looks at how technology may support onboarding, routine administration and employee-record workflows. Its emphasis is operational: understanding where automation can assist and where human review remains necessary.

Study Talent Acquisition Management for AI-supported recruitment

Talent Acquisition Management concentrates on the use of AI in sourcing, screening and candidate evaluation. It is distinct from the Recruitment Skills course because it focuses on technology-assisted methods rather than the wider structure and practice of recruitment.

What to Expect from Free HR Study

These short courses provide introductory learning for personal or professional development. They can help you build subject awareness, recognise common HR processes and decide whether you would like to study the field in greater depth.

HR practice depends on an organisation's policies and the employment law that applies in its location. Course content should not be treated as legal advice. When AI or automation is used in recruitment or employee administration, organisations should also consider privacy, fairness, accuracy, security and appropriate human oversight.

Completion does not confer professional HR status, guarantee employment or replace a qualification required by an employer or professional body. Check the requirements of your intended role before making a study decision.

Free Study and Optional Certificates

Enrolment and access to the learning materials are free for the courses listed on this page. A certificate is not included automatically and is not required to complete the free study.

After successfully completing an eligible course, learners may choose to purchase either a CPD-accredited certificate issued by the CPD Standards Office or a certificate endorsed by the Quality Licence Scheme. The options and fees differ according to the course level, certificate type and delivery choice. Review the current certificate options and prices before ordering.

An optional certificate records course completion; it is not an Ofqual-regulated qualification and should not be presented as one.

When to Consider a Longer HR Programme

A 20-hour course is useful for exploring one subject, but it cannot provide the depth of a longer programme. If you want broader coverage, more detailed assessment or a clearly stated course level, compare the full range of HR courses. Read each course page carefully to confirm its level, study hours, assessment, endorsement or regulated status, fees and certificate arrangements.

If you are still exploring the field, the guide to what human resources involves explains the function of HR and the types of responsibilities commonly associated with it.

Who Can Enrol?

These courses are designed for flexible, independent study and may suit:

  • beginners considering HR as a field of study;
  • employees who want a clearer understanding of people-management processes;
  • small-business owners and team leaders seeking introductory HR knowledge;
  • recruitment or administrative staff developing related skills; and
  • learners interested in how AI is being applied to HR operations and talent acquisition.

No previous HR experience is required for these introductory courses. Learners should nevertheless check the details on the individual course page before enrolling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the HR courses really free?

Yes. Enrolment and online access to the course materials are free. Optional certificates are paid products.

How long does each course take?

Each course listed on this page has an estimated study time of 20 hours. Because study is self-paced, the time taken to finish will depend on your schedule and pace.

Which course is best for an HR beginner?

The HR Management Short Course offers the broadest introduction. Recruitment Skills is more focused on hiring, while the two AI-related courses examine specific uses of technology in HR and talent acquisition.

Will I receive a free certificate?

No. Certification is optional and subject to a fee. You can complete the learning without purchasing a certificate.

Are these regulated HR qualifications?

No. The free short courses listed here are not Ofqual-regulated qualifications. The status of any longer or regulated programme should be confirmed on its individual course page.

Will completing a course qualify me for an HR job?

No course can guarantee a job or professional status. These courses provide introductory learning; employers and professional bodies determine their own education and experience requirements.
